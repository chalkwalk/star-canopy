# Macros

The macros are the controls. Each runs from -1 to 1 between two words for the sky,
and 0 is the seed's own sky. Set them in the project's `[macros]`, which
`starcanopy new` fills with every macro at 0, or on the command line with
`--macro NAME=VALUE`.

| Macro | -1 | 1 | What it moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` | enveloping | open | how much of the sky the gas covers |
| `dense` | tenuous | dense | how much the gas hides where it is |
| `fragmented` | whole | fragmented | gas broken into pieces with clear sky between |
| `detailed` | smooth | detailed | fine structure in the gas |
| `billowing` | wispy | billowing | rounded billows, or ridged filaments |
| `crisp` | soft | crisp | hard edges and hard shadows |
| `turbulent` | calm | turbulent | gas buckled, swirled and blown out |
| `luminous` | brooding | luminous | light from within the nebula |
| `bright` | dim | bright | the whole sky, as an exposure |
| `hazy` | clear | hazy | a glow over the sky, the dark between not black |
| `vivid` | muted | vivid | how colourful |
| `starry` | sparse | starry | how many stars, and how bright |
| `galactic` | remote | galactic | where in the galaxy: in toward its centre, or far out |

Macros add: two that move the same dial move it together. Every name has been
confirmed blind -- picked as the side it names on the skies it was scored on --
though how far each end goes may still change.
